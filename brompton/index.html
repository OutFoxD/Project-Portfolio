You use 1st and 6th on your M6L: Brompton BWR 6-speed

Most range (302%) of anything that fits your rear frame, and every part is stock Brompton, so any dealer can service it. Adding a third rear cog later gives 9 speeds (≈350%) for about 20 g. If you'd strip your current M6L, its rear wheel and shifters are exactly these parts.

9.69.3 light
You mostly ride the middle gears: Brompton 2-speed + Superlight front wheel

The lightest build that is still all-stock and close to maintenance-free. About 0.75 kg lighter than the BWR. Range is only ≈135%, which is flat-city gearing.

5-speed derailleurThe BrommiePlus Hubsmith wheelsets are the lightest proper option (≈9.4–9.5 kg), but a 5-cog cassette only gives ≈190% range, less than the Sturmey 5-speed or BWR, and the derailleur parts are third-party pieces made mainly for the newer P/T Line frame. If you go this way, take the R002 set (120 kg rated) over the Kinlin (85 kg limit).
Sturmey 5-speedOne shifter and 256% range, but about as heavy as the BWR, and BrommiePlus itself warns it is hard to keep adjusted. No real gain over the BWR for your goals.
Carbon wheelsAbout the same weight as the Kinlin set (estimated; the weight isn't listed), but with Brompton rim calipers braking on a carbon track. Poor trade for a daily bike.
Carbon forkA Brompton titanium fork weighs about the same (260–350 g). Titanium bends before it fails; an unbranded carbon fork can snap without warning. If you keep the carbon fork, see the checklist below.
Not an optionBrompton's own 4- and 12-speed systems need the newer Advance rear frame. Your titanium rear frame is the older Superlight/C Line type.

Before you order

  • Carbon fork fit. The RIRO listing mentions 355 as well as 349. Ask the seller where the brake bridge sits: if it's placed for 355 rims, a Brompton caliper may not reach a 349 rim. Also confirm steerer length and thread match your main frame's headset, and that the fold hook and mudguard mounts are there.
  • Derailleur on the old rear frame. Most 5–7-speed Brompton derailleur kits are made for the P/T Line Advance frame and fit a C Line frame "after modification". Ask BrommiePlus which hanger and derailleur fit a Superlight Ti rear frame before buying a Hubsmith wheelset.
  • Chain tensioner type. BWR and 2-speed need the derailleur-version tensioner and chain pusher; the Sturmey 5-speed needs the non-derailleur tensioner.
  • Rider weight. The Kinlin wheelset is rated for riders under 85 kg; the R002 set passed ISO tests at 120 kg.
  • Import costs. BrommiePlus ships from Taiwan: expect 20% import VAT plus up to 4.7% duty on top of their price. AliExpress shows VAT included but warns that import charges still apply.
  • Weigh as you go. Put each part on a kitchen scale when it arrives and replace the estimates here with real numbers; the fork and carbon wheels have no published weight.

How the weights are estimated

The reference is a factory Brompton M2E-X: steel main frame and M stem, titanium fork and rear frame, 2-speed, no mudguards or rack, reported at about 9.7 kg. Your frame matches it part for part, so each option is added as a change against that bike's stock wheels and gear parts. Two other factory bikes agree with that reference: the S6E-X based CHPT3 edition at 10.3 kg with light tyres, and the gap between Brompton's 2-speed and 6-speed systems of about 0.73 kg.
