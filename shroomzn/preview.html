All four services count a day's rain from 06:00 UTC to 06:00 UTC the next day, and every country's presets end on the same day. Station sums and the rain grids are precomputed a few times a day; the 24 h view for Austria and Switzerland and Austrian custom ranges outside the cache query the services live (GeoSphere limit: 240 requests/hour); the 24 h view for Germany and Czechia is refreshed every 3 hours. Custom ranges outside Austria cover the last 45 days.
